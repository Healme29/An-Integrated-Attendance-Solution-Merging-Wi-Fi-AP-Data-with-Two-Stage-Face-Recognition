## Decision (2026-10-24)

- **Chosen threshold: keep `FACE_SIMILARITY_THRESHOLD = 0.5`** (no config change needed).
- Measured distributions (2 students, 8 samples): genuine pairs n=13
  (min **0.708**, max 0.948, mean 0.838); impostor pairs n=15
  (min 0.062, **max 0.224**, mean 0.169). Any threshold in **[0.224, 0.708]**
  yields FAR = 0 and FRR = 0 — 0.5 sits in the middle of that safe gap.
- Why NOT 0.25 (the script's suggestion): it minimizes FAR+FRR but sits only
  0.026 above the measured impostor max (0.224) — far too little margin. For
  classroom attendance an accepted impostor is worse than a false reject
  (wrong person gets attendance vs. student retries), so the midpoint of the
  gap is the robust choice.
