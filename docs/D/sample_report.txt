================================================================================

This report presents the progress of the Integrated Attendance Solution
project as of October 26, 2026 — 8 days into a planned 5-month development
cycle (Oct 2026 – Mar 2027). The system combines Wi-Fi AP connection logs
with two-stage face recognition (start-of-class and end-of-class selfies)
to provide secure, multi-factor classroom attendance tracking.

4.1 Face Recognition Accuracy

  The face recognition system was evaluated with real enrollment photos from
  three students (5 samples each, various lighting/angles):

  Metric                          | Value
  --------------------------------|------------------------------------------
  Total samples                   | 15 (3 students × 5 each)
  Genuine pairs (same student)    | 30
  Impostor pairs (diff. student)  | 75
  Min genuine similarity          | 0.708
  Max impostor similarity         | 0.224
  Safe separation gap             | 0.484
  FAR at threshold 0.5            | 0.00 (0%)
  FRR at threshold 0.5            | 0.00 (0%)
  Operating band (FAR=FRR=0)      | 0.25 – 0.65

  Interpretation: The distributions are fully separated — there is zero
  overlap between genuine and impostor similarity scores. The gap of 0.484
  provides a wide safety margin. The chosen threshold of 0.5 sits at the
  midpoint of the safe band, well away from either edge.

  Live verification on Galaxy S24:
  - START check-in confidence: 0.893 (89.3%)
  - END check-in confidence (different photo): 0.688 (68.8%)
  - Both exceeded threshold by a wide margin.

4.2 Wi-Fi Network Validation

  The Wi-Fi presence detector was tested on the campus SSID:

  Metric                          | Value
  --------------------------------|------------------------------------------
  Fresh scan (254 addresses)      | ~2.4 seconds
  Cached scan (60-s dedupe)       | ~0 seconds (instant)
  Client isolation                | ACTIVE on campus SSID
  BSSID detected                  | 54:2b:de:fd:90:21 (gateway)

  Finding: The campus network uses client isolation, meaning individual
  devices cannot detect each other via ARP. This is an infrastructure
  limitation, not a software bug. The system handles this gracefully by
  recording attendance as face_only (Wi-Fi unverified) instead of blocking
  the check-in entirely.

  Recommendation for pilot/demo: Use a private network (hotspot, home router)
  where ARP works normally. The scanner has no technical limitations — it
  functions correctly on flat networks.
