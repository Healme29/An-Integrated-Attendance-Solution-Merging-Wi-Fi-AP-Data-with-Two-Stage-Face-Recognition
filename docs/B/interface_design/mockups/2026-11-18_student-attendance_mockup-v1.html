02 / HOW IT DECIDES

A selfie alone is not enough. The device must be on the classroom network, the scan must sit inside the class window, and both faces must match. In strict mode, an off-network device gets a 403 and leaves no row.

Wi-Fi evidence is AP-coverage level (L2), not proof that a person sat in the room. The copy here deliberately avoids saying otherwise. The app checks /schedules/active and /attendance/preflight before the camera opens, so a blocked check-in never reaches the selfie step.
