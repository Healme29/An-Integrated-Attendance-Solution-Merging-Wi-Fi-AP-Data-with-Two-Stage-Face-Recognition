02 / HOW IT DECIDES

A selfie alone is not enough. The device must be on the school network, the scanned token must belong to the current phase, and the face must match the logged-in student. A phase change issues a new token, so an old QR stops working immediately.

01 · Network02 · QR token03 · Face + liveness04 · Identity bound

The token rotates every 5 s and the previous one stays valid for 10 s. The roster collapses check-ins into verified, partial, pending or absent. This is a subnet gate, not room-level proof; the demo runs on a hotspot the team controls.
